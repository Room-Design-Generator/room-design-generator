## How to Use (3 Steps + Photo Tips)

1. **Upload a room photo** (JPG, PNG, or WebP up to 12 MB — desktop or mobile browser; convert iPhone HEIC to JPG first) or start with a built-in sample.
2. **Pick your room type and style**, and optionally note items to keep.
3. **Generate, compare before vs. after, and download** the redesign you want to keep.

For the most faithful proportions, see our [5-Minute Room Photography Guide](https://roomdesigngenerator.com/how-to-take-room-photos):

- **Stand in the doorway or a corner** at chest height using your phone's standard `1x` lens.
- **Turn on room lights and open curtains**, avoiding pointing straight into harsh window glare.
- **Clear the floor first** (bags, cables, laundry, pets) so clutter isn't redrawn as furniture.

## Practical Use Cases & Honest Limitations

### Where it helps

- Testing a lighter sofa, rug, or wall colour before spending money
- Exploring renter-friendly refreshes (textiles, lighting, mirrors, plants) with zero drilling
- Narrowing saved inspiration boards by running the same room through two or three styles side by side
- Spotting bulky furniture pieces when planning a move or declutter

### Clear boundaries

- **Visual direction, not a floor plan:** always measure wall width, rug size, and door clearance before ordering furniture.
- **No shopping links or budget estimates:** use the generated image as a visual reference when shopping or testing paint samples.
- **Cannot add natural daylight:** brighter renders illustrate how pale paint and lamps help, not a larger window.
- **Label concepts honestly:** if used in a property listing or client proposal, clearly label AI redesigns as visual concepts.
